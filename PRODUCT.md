# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A group of adult and teen friends at a one-time party night, gathered around one laptop or TV. One person drives the laptop; everyone else watches from a sofa, a few meters away. Roasting each other is part of the fun.

## Product Purpose

"دوبلاج" is a party game: press a button to get a random animated-movie clip, watch the original, then one or more players dub it live into a mic. The clip immediately replays with their voices over the original music and effects. Success is laughter, and a final best-of reel everyone wants to rewatch.

## Positioning

The dub plays back instantly and in sync, over the real soundtrack with the original voices removed by AI. It feels like a real dubbing booth, with no editing step.

## Operating Context

- Played once, in one evening, on a laptop or TV, in Chrome or Edge, from a local server.
- Clips: Disney / Pixar classics, 10-25 seconds, supplied by the host into `clips/`.
- Players record with headphones on; one mic for everyone.
- Modes: with voting and points (non-dubbers vote 1-10, the average goes to each dubber), or casual with no points.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static `index.html` + `prepare.py` (Demucs voice removal, thumbnails, `clips.json`) + `serve.py` (range-capable local server).
- Flow: setup (players, mode, mic test) → home (round, random button, leaderboard) → slot-machine reveal → pick dubbers → watch original → 3-2-1 record → instant dub playback (volume, sync, redo, save as video) → vote → final podium and best-of reel.
- Clip filename `(N)` means N voices; a sidecar `.txt` holds lines shown on screen.
- Recordings live in memory only; a page refresh loses them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: دوبلاج.
- Interface language: Arabic, right-to-left, Gulf dialect, playful and jokey.
- Must be readable from a sofa on a TV.

## Evidence on Hand

No real clips are in the project yet; the host adds them. Do not invent movie titles as if they were in the library.

## Product Principles

- The clip is the star; the interface frames it and steps aside while it plays.
- Every step reads at a glance from across a room: one obvious action per screen.
- Ceremony beats efficiency: reveals, countdowns and results are moments, not transitions.
- Never lose a take: recording, playback and scoring must stay reliable over any effect.
## Remote-seat extension (2026-10-21)

The owner can now join one remote seat while friends share a TV laptop. The hosted static copy is generated by `export.py`; all clip preparation and editing remain local. Supabase Broadcast/Presence carries room messages and Storage carries takes. No accounts, score persistence, or TV mirroring were added. A remote player always dubs alone, using the same local capture routine as the host, and can vote remotely on local takes. Missing configuration leaves remote controls hidden. Deployment setup belongs to the owner; follow `DEPLOY.md`.
